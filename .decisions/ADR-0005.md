# ADR-0005: Funnel definitions are global, results are evaluated per session

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Category:** Product / Scope
- **Status:** Superseded
- **Importance:** normal
- **Superseded by:** ADR-0011

## Context

Concept Q7: global or per-session funnel definitions.

## Decision

Funnels are stored globally in chrome.storage.local and evaluated against a selected session; results are computed on read, never stored.

## Consequences

One funnel can compare several sessions; the Funnel view always names the evaluated session.
